Windows 11 · WPF · open source

The top edge of your screen was doing nothing.

MajikUtils hangs a small island from it. Glance at it to see what is playing. Click it and it becomes the one place you type — a task, a timer, a sum, a search across everything your machine already knows.

One box. What you type decides what it becomes.

There is no mode to pick and nothing to click first. A line under the box tells you what Enter is about to do, before you press it — so the grammar is something you notice rather than something you learn.

You typeWhat happens
renew the domainA task, to tick off later.
25m or 1h30A countdown, with a ring on the island.
@9am call TomA countdown to nine, which says call Tom when it fires.
23 * 1.2Shows 27.6. Enter copies it.
pomA focus cycle: 25 on, 5 off, a long break every fourth.
.wifi is on the routerA note.
/firefoxSearches apps, folder stacks, recent files and clipboard.
It never eats what you typed. @home buy milk is a task, because what follows the at-sign is not a clock. buy 2 x 4 timber is a task, because the calculator refuses anything it cannot read whole. A grammar that swallows a sentence is worse than one that occasionally under-reads it.

Twenty-five minutes, and then it tells you.

pom starts the cycle and the island runs it: focus, a short break, a longer one every fourth round, advancing on its own the whole way. Four dots fill as you earn them, so the set reads at a glance without any arithmetic.

The lengths are not adjustable, on purpose. 25/5/15 is what the technique is. A pomodoro you have to set up first is a timer with extra steps, and the island already has a very good timer.

A clipboard that keeps the screenshot.

Text, images and copied files. A screenshot is the thing on that list you most cannot get back — take a second one and the first is gone — so images are held and shown as themselves, and drag straight out into whatever wants them.

Only what you pin is written down. The rest of the history lives in memory and dies with the app. A clipboard sees passwords, licence keys and one-time codes, and quietly keeping all of them in a file between sessions would be a liability sold as a convenience. Pinning is you saying keep this — and a pin survives the list filling up, Clear, and a restart.

Seven places, one keystroke apart.

The lit scope says its name; the rest stay out of the way as icons. Arrow keys and Enter work throughout, so a search never needs the mouse.

  • Capture

    The box, and a feed of everything you have put through it.

  • Shelf

    Drag a file at the island and it opens ready to drop into.

  • Clipboard

    Text, images and files, searchable and pinnable.

  • Apps

    Everything installed, plus anything winget can fetch.

  • Recent

    What you had open, without going to find it.

  • Stacks

    A folder fanned out from its own taskbar button.

  • Mixer

    Per-application volume, where the sound is coming from.

It gets out of the way.

The island never takes focus until you ask it to, lets clicks pass straight through whenever its controls are not showing, and takes itself off screen when a game or a full-screen video owns the monitor. Pointing at it opens it. Clicking holds it open until you click away or press Esc.

The colour comes off the album art. The progress bar, the lit scope and the focused input all take their hue from whatever is playing — the same pair of colours the four bars in the pill have always used.

Put something on the edge.
